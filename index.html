About the Firm

A specialized brokerage built on trust and hospitality expertise.

Amin Realty, Inc. is a commercial real estate brokerage focused exclusively on the hospitality sector. From single independent motels to multi-property franchise portfolios, we represent buyers and sellers with the confidentiality and diligence that high-value transactions demand. Our reach extends nationwide, and our relationships span private owners, institutional investors, and international capital.

  • Access to private, off-market listings across the country.
  • Specialists in hospitality — independent & franchise hotels and motels.
  • Guidance for complex structures — 1031 Exchange, EB-5, and REIT acquisitions.
