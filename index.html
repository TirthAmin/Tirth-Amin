Questions

Frequently asked questions

Answers to what owners and investors ask us most. Don't see yours? Get in touch.

What types of properties do you handle?
We specialize in hospitality real estate — independent motels and franchise hotels — along with land for new hospitality and commercial development, distressed assets, and select commercial properties. Our focus is the hotel and motel sector nationwide.
Do you represent buyers, sellers, or both?
Both. We represent owners looking to sell discreetly and investors looking to acquire cash-flowing, value-add, or distressed assets. Representation terms are always established in writing — see our Information About Brokerage Services notice.
What is an off-market or private listing?
Many hospitality owners prefer to sell without public marketing to protect staff, guests, and operations. We maintain access to private inventory that is never advertised publicly. Contact us to discuss what may be available in your target market.
Can you help with a 1031 Exchange or EB-5 investment?
Yes. We regularly coordinate with qualified intermediaries on 1031 tax-deferred exchanges and source projects suitable for EB-5 and international buyers. We are a brokerage, not a tax or legal advisor, so we work alongside your own professionals.
What areas do you serve?
We are based in Sugar Land, Texas and serve clients across the United States, having worked with investors spanning multiple states. Reach out to discuss your specific market.
