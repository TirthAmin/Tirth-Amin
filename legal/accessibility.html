Accessibility Statement

Last updated: [VERIFY-DATE]

Amin Realty, Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

Our Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, and they are the standard most commonly referenced under the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Measures We Take

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper headings and landmark regions.
  • A “Skip to main content” link for keyboard and screen-reader users.
  • Full keyboard operability with clearly visible focus indicators.
  • Text and interactive elements that meet AA color-contrast ratios.
  • Descriptive text alternatives for meaningful images and icons.
  • Labelled form fields and accessible, descriptive link text.
  • Respect for the operating-system “reduce motion” preference, which disables non-essential animation.
  • A responsive layout that supports zoom up to 200% without loss of content.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We periodically review the website and address issues as we identify them. Some third-party content or integrations (such as embedded maps or forms) may not be fully under our control; where possible we select accessible providers.

Tip: Before launch, validate the live site with an automated checker (for example WAVE or Lighthouse) and, ideally, a manual screen-reader pass. Re-test whenever you change hosting platforms (GoDaddy / Wix), since platform wrappers can affect accessibility.
