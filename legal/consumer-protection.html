TREC Consumer Protection Notice

Texas Real Estate Commission · Official notice required to be displayed by Texas brokers.

Action required before launch: This reproduces the standard TREC Consumer Protection Notice. Confirm the current wording against trec.texas.gov and link the official PDF if you prefer. This notice must be readily accessible from your homepage.

The Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) regulates real estate brokers and sales agents, real estate inspectors, home warranty companies, easement and right-of-way agents, and timeshare interest providers.

You can find more information and check the license status of a Texas real estate broker or sales agent by visiting www.trec.texas.gov.

You may also contact the Texas Real Estate Commission:

  • Texas Real Estate Commission
  • P.O. Box 12188, Austin, Texas 78711-2188
  • Phone: (512) 936-3000
  • Website: www.trec.texas.gov

If you have a complaint regarding a real estate license holder, you may file it with TREC. Information about filing a complaint and about the agency's recovery fund (the Real Estate Recovery Trust Account) is available on the TREC website.
